# Citation Styles Guide

Quick reference for major scientific citation styles (APA, AMA, Vancouver, Chicago, IEEE).

## 1. AMA (American Medical Association)

Standard for medical, health, and biological sciences. Uses numbered superscript citations.

**In-text citation:**
The incidence of [condition] has increased significantly over the past decade.²
As noted by Smith and colleagues,¹⁵...

**Reference list:**
1. Author AA, Author BB. Title of article. *Abbreviated Journal Name*. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages.
2. Smith JD, Jones BC. Randomized trial of treatment X. *JAMA*. 2023;329(14):1152-1160. doi:10.1001/jama.2023.1234
3. Doe RL. *Clinical Manual of Medicine*. 5th ed. Publisher; Year.

## 2. Vancouver

Common in medicine and biomedical sciences. Uses numbered citations in square brackets or parentheses.

**In-text citation:**
Recent evidence suggests a strong correlation [1].
Studies [2, 5-8] have shown...

**Reference list:**
1. Author AA, Author BB. Title of article. Abbreviated Journal Name. Year Month Day;Volume(Issue):Pages.
2. Smith JD, Jones BC. Outcomes after intervention Y. N Engl J Med. 2024 Jan 15;390(3):215-224.
3. World Health Organization. Global health report 2023. Geneva: WHO; 2023.

## 3. APA (American Psychological Association)

Standard for social sciences, psychology, and education. Uses author-date citations.

**In-text citation:**
(Smith & Jones, 2023)
Smith and Jones (2023) argued that...
(Lee et al., 2022) -- *Use "et al." for 3 or more authors*

**Reference list:**
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Journal*, *Volume*(Issue), Pages. https://doi.org/xxxx
Smith, J. D., & Jones, B. C. (2023). Psychological impacts of intervention X. *Journal of Applied Psychology*, *58*(2), 145-162.

## 4. Chicago Style

Common in humanities and sciences. Support both Notes/Bibliography and Author/Date systems.

**In-text (Author-Date):**
(Smith 2023, 145)
Smith (2023) observes that...

**Reference list:**
Author, Firstname. Year. "Title of Article." *Journal Name* Volume, no. Issue (Month/Season): Pages.
Smith, John. 2023. "A History of Innovation." *Scientific History Quarterly* 12, no. 3 (Fall): 45-68.

## 5. IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)

Standard for engineering and computer science. Uses numbered citations in square brackets.

**In-text citation:**
As shown in [1], the algorithm...
Several methods have been proposed [2], [5], [8]--[10].

**Reference list:**
[1] A. A. Author and B. B. Author, "Title of paper," *Abbreviated Journal Name*, vol. X, no. X, pp. XXX-XXX, Month, Year.
[2] J. D. Smith and B. C. Jones, "Optimizing neural networks," *IEEE Trans. Neural Netw.*, vol. 34, no. 5, pp. 1120-1135, May 2024.

---

## Citation Best Practices

1. **Verify all citations**: Ensure every in-text citation has a corresponding entry in the reference list.
2. **Cite primary sources**: Always prefer citing the original research paper rather than reviews or news articles.
3. **Be consistent**: Choose one style and apply it strictly throughout the entire document.
4. **Use stable identifiers**: Always include DOIs (preferred) or stable URLs for all references.
5. **Check recentness**: Prioritize research from the last 5-10 years unless citing historical foundational work.
6. **Balance**: Avoid "citation clumps" (10 citations for one sentence) unless necessary; spread citations to support specific claims.
